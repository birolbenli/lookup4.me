About

lookup4.me is a small collection of practical DNS, email authentication and SSL tools. The goal is a clean interface: open a tool, enter a domain or IP, get a clear result.

Current tools include MX, SPF, DKIM (common selector detection), DMARC, bulk SSL checks, reverse DNS and SMTP port 25 connectivity tests. More tools may be added over time.

Built by Birol Benli. If you find it useful, you can buy me a coffee.
