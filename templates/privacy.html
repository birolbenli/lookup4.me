Privacy & redaction guide

lookup4.me tools are meant for technical debugging. Do not paste secrets, customer data, or anything you would not want stored or logged by mistake. You are responsible for every value you submit.

Important: Email headers and full message sources can contain personal names, addresses, internal hostnames, tracking IDs, authentication tokens, and business content. Redact before pasting whenever possible.

What to remove or mask

  • Email addresses of people (keep only domains if needed): j***@example.com
  • Names in From / To / Cc display names
  • Subjects that reveal private topics — replace with [REDACTED]
  • Message bodies — prefer headers-only unless body analysis is required
  • Cookies / tokens / API keys in custom headers (Authorization, X-*, tracking pixels)
  • Internal IPs / hostnames if they are sensitive in your environment
  • DKIM / ARC signatures are usually fine to keep for auth checks; body content is not

Quick redaction method

  1. Open the message source (“Show original”).
  2. Copy into a local editor (Notepad, VS Code, etc.).
  3. Search and replace personal emails, names, and subject text with placeholders.
  4. Delete everything after the first blank line if you only need headers.
  5. Paste the cleaned text into the Header Analyzer.

Example

From: Alice Example <a***@company.com>
To: Bob <b***@client.com>
Subject: [REDACTED]
Message-ID: <keep-or-mask@mail.company.com>
DKIM-Signature: ... (ok to keep)
Authentication-Results: ... (ok to keep)

[body removed]

Our expectations

  • Do not submit credentials, private keys, session cookies, or confidential documents.
  • Mail Tester addresses are temporary; still avoid sending sensitive production mail.
  • If you paste something by mistake, treat it as exposed and rotate any secrets involved.
